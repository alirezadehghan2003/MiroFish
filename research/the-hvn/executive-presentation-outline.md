**Slide 1: Title**
"Making The HVN easy to trust and easy to buy". Strategic intelligence review, October 2026.

**Slide 2: What we did (method in one slide)**
- Full live crawl of the-hvn.com (216 pages), tiered competitor scan, 6 MiroFish simulations with 10–12 fictional client personas.
- Total AI cost: **$0.71**. No customer data used.
- Simulations are **hypotheses**; validation with real clients is step 12 of the plan.

**Slide 3: The headline**
- The HVN's proposition is distinctive: **doctor-led team, one address, published prices, no membership** [V].
- It is undersold: buyers can't see **who treats them, what a plan costs, or what "CQC registered" means** [V + S].

**Slide 4: Three things to fix this month** [V]
1. The Menopause, PCOS and PMDD pages show acne treatments.
2. Two menu links on every page are broken; 19 broken links in total.
3. The programme pages (£14.5k / £8k / £12k) are gone; the functional-medicine link lands on an unfinished page.

**Slide 5: The biggest conversion barrier: "price fog"**
- Consultations £175–£400 and tests up to £750 are priced; programmes are not (except 8R from £3,000) [V].
- Simulated clients: "I won't pay a consultation fee just to find out the total" (7/11), but "a range in writing is enough" [S].
- **Ask:** publish indicative ranges, a fee-credit table and the cancellation charge in £.

**Slide 6: Trust = verifiability**
- Every page shows the same 4 practitioner names; the CQC badge links nowhere (0/212); "London's leading" on every page [V].
- Simulated clients asked: who treats me, is it inspected, what happens in the 10-minute IV check, what is the evidence [S, 5 runs].
- **Ask:** "Who will treat me" blocks, a CQC status line ("first inspection pending"), a drip explainer, and claims review.

**Slide 7: Competitive position**
- Rivals sell membership (Surrenne ~£10k + £5k; Lanesborough ~£4–6k; HUM2N monthly) [R].
- In simulation, **"no membership" was a plus for 10 of 11** ("no lock-in") [S].
- The HVN loses to **specialists** for single needs (menopause, fertility) and to **HUM2N** for data-led optimisers, until programmes are priced and the inspection is complete [S].

**Slide 8: Recommended positioning**
> "Doctor-led care for skin, energy and long-term health: one Knightsbridge team, one plan, clear prices, no membership."
- Retire the superlatives; lead with people, prices and honesty about evidence.

**Slide 9: SWOT on a page**
- **S:** integrated doctor-led team, price transparency, no lock-in, women's-health doctor.
- **W:** price fog, broken pages, not yet inspected, generic team display, claim wording.
- **O:** the transparent alternative to clubs, women's-health hub, an evidence-honest longevity offer.
- **T:** new 2026 longevity entrants, £15.5k programme anchors, specialists, ASA scrutiny.

**Slide 10: 30 / 60 / 90 days**
- **30 days:** fix women's-health pages and broken links; CQC line + link; one phone number; claims review; fee-credit table; mobile call button.
- **60 days:** "Who will treat me"; programme price ranges; drip explainer; new proposition line; schema; client interviews and funnel baseline.
- **90 days:** launch positioning; pilot a priced first-step pathway; women's-health and fertility hub; re-test messaging; feature the CQC rating once issued.

**Slide 11: Limitations (be explicit)**
- Simulated personas ≠ customers; one AI model; cautious voice bias; seed-dependent; competitor data press-sourced.
- Fabricated agent "experiences" were removed; the alternative-tagline test did not run properly and will be repeated.

**Slide 12: Decisions requested today**
1. Approve the 30-day fix list (low cost, high certainty).
2. Agree to publish programme price ranges (needs finance input).
3. Adopt the positioning direction for testing.
4. Commission the claims review.
5. Approve 8–12 client interviews to validate before any paid campaign.
