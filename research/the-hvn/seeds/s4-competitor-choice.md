# Briefing S4: Where would you go? (The HVN vs London alternatives)

Briefing for a simulated online discussion comparing a real London clinic, The HVN, with real alternatives, using only public information gathered on or before 15 October 2026. It contains no customer data. Participants are invented archetypes.

Labels: [Published] = The HVN's live website, 15 Oct 2026. [Reported] = press releases, trade press or directories found by search; **competitor websites could not be loaded directly, so competitor details may be out of date or incomplete**. **Facts to keep straight (read before posting):**
- **The HVN does not currently offer any membership.** It sells individual consultations, tests and treatments, plus the 8R programme "from £3,000" [Published, 15 Oct 2026].
- **CQC registration is not an inspection.** The HVN is registered with the Care Quality Commission, but the register listed it as **not yet inspected and therefore unrated** (October 2026) [Reported]. Registration does not mean the clinic has been inspected.
- Competitor details come from press, press releases or directories and **may be out of date** [Reported]. No participant has visited any competitor unless their description says so, and nobody should invent experiences of real clinics.
- Anything marked HYPOTHETICAL or TEST MESSAGE is **not** a real HVN offer or real HVN wording.

## Part A: The HVN [Published unless marked]
- Knightsbridge (57–63 Knightsbridge). Open 7 days, until 21:30 Monday to Saturday.
- **One site, five service families:** aesthetics, Traditional Chinese Medicine, IV infusions, blood testing and longevity, and spa-style therapies such as the Hydrobed.
- **Team:** GP-trained Medical Director, a second doctor covering aesthetics and women's health, a senior acupuncturist, an osteopath, an aesthetic nurse and therapists.
- **No membership.** Pay per visit, with most prices published:
  - aesthetic consultation £175; Discovery Consultation £350; 8R longevity consultation £400;
  - blood profiles £450 / £550; HRU assessment £750;
  - IV £275–£425; NAD+ £350–£800; acupuncture from £200; injections from £260;
  - 8R Longevity Programme (with partner clinic Levitas) from £3,000; other programme prices not published.
- **Trust:** CQC registered, but not yet inspected and so unrated [Reported, Oct 2026]; Google reviews shown on site; 24-hour cancellation with card on file.

## Part B: Alternatives

### Directly comparable, Knightsbridge
- **Surrenne** (at The Emory hotel, Knightsbridge; opened April 2024) [Reported]:
  - members-only wellbeing club with a spa, a celebrity-trainer studio and a medically led longevity lounge (blood work, VO2 max, microbiome and epigenetic testing);
  - science advisory board reportedly includes well-known longevity researchers;
  - membership reported at £10,000 a year plus a £5,000 joining fee, capped at about 100 members.
- **The Lanesborough Club & Spa** (Hyde Park Corner) [Reported]:
  - heritage-hotel members' club and spa with a pool and gym, aesthetics and regenerative facials (about £500);
  - membership reported at roughly £4,000–£6,000 a year (figures conflict);
  - no current longevity programme found.
- **The Wellness Clinic at Harrods** (same street) [Reported]:
  - independent operators under one roof: aesthetics doctors, dermatology, an IV-drip brand, dental;
  - examples: facial consultation £195, Profhilo from £800 (some prices years old);
  - Swiss longevity brand consultations reported in 2023.

### Longevity specialists elsewhere in London
- **HUM2N** (Chelsea; also inside Six Senses London since June 2026) [Reported]: doctor-led longevity, IV, hyperbaric oxygen, cryotherapy. Monthly programmes about £495–£695 a month plus a one-off fee; NAD+ £295–£450.
- **Lanserhof at The Arts Club** (Mayfair) [Reported]: medical gym and clinic with a separate membership, about £6,500 a year plus joining (2022 figure; other figures conflict).
- **Pillar Wellbeing at Raffles London at The OWO** [Reported]: hotel wellbeing club; 12-week transformation programme from £15,500 (2026).
- **Hooke London** (Mayfair) [Reported]: imaging-heavy diagnostics; up to £54,000 a year, or about £15,000 for a one-day assessment (2025).
- **The Online GP** (Marylebone) [Reported]: diagnostics-led longevity testing; panels from about £595–£1,400.

### Specialists for one service
- **Taktouk Clinic** (Sloane Street) [Reported]: award-winning aesthetics clinic, CQC rated Good (2023), price on request.
- **Specialist menopause clinics**: consultations about £185–£275 [Reported, directory].
- **Specialist fertility acupuncture clinics**: sessions from about £90 [Reported, directory].

[Hypothesis] The HVN is the only option above that combines aesthetics, acupuncture, IV, testing and spa therapies at one address without a membership, and publishes most prices. Rivals compete on exclusivity (Surrenne), heritage (Lanesborough, Harrods), medical-longevity depth (HUM2N, Lanserhof, Hooke) or specialist focus.
