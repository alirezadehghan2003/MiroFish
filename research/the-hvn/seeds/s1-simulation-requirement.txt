Simulate a Reddit-style discussion in a London luxury-wellness community about The HVN, a real Knightsbridge clinic, using only the attached briefing. The agents are the twelve invented discussion participants in Part D; the clinic, its staff, competitors and regulators are background facts, not participants. The thread starts with the question in Part C.

Research questions: (1) Do participants see The HVN as a spa, a medical clinic, a longevity clinic or an aesthetics clinic, and how confused are they? (2) Which facts build trust and which erode it (medical team, CQC registration without a rating, reviews, press, published prices, IV benefit claims, unpublished programme prices, two phone numbers)? (3) Which cues read as luxury and which do not? (4) How does The HVN compare with Surrenne, the Lanesborough, the Harrods clinic and HUM2N for each participant? (5) What would make each participant book, and what would stop them?

Rules: participants must stay in character, use only facts from the briefing, say when they are unsure, and never invent prices, offers, reviews, outcomes or personal experiences of treatments they have not had. Prices may be out of date. This is a hypothesis-generating exercise, not market research.
