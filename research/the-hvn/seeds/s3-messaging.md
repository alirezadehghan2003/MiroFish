# Briefing S3: How do The HVN's website messages land? (Messaging, discovery, claims)

Briefing for a simulated online discussion. It describes a real London clinic, The HVN, using only public information checked on 15 October 2026. It contains no customer data. Participants are invented archetypes.

Labels: [Published] = quoted from The HVN's live website on 15 Oct 2026. [Reported] = third-party or regulator; may be out of date. [Hypothesis] = analyst inference. [TEST MESSAGE] = alternative wording invented for this simulation; **The HVN has not published it**. [Invented] = fictional participant.

**Important limitation:** participants cannot browse the website. This simulation tests reactions to *wording*, not usability. Website usability is assessed separately in a real audit.

## Part A: Context in brief
The HVN is a Knightsbridge clinic offering medical aesthetics, Traditional Chinese Medicine, IV infusions, blood testing and longevity programmes, and spa therapies, with a GP-trained Medical Director [Published]. It is registered with the Care Quality Commission but not yet inspected [Reported, Oct 2026]. Most services show a price on their page [Published].

## Part B: What the website actually says [Published, verbatim excerpts]

1. **Line repeated on every page:** "LONDON'S LEADING WELLNESS & LONGEVITY CLINIC".
2. **Home page rotating banners:** "NOW OFFERING ACUPUNCTURE WITH [the senior TCM practitioner]"; "REGENERATIVE AESTHETICS IN KNIGHTSBRIDGE"; "DOCTOR-LED FOTONA LASER 5D FACE LIFT". Main heading: "HOW THE HVN CAN HELP".
3. **About page:** "At The HVN, wellness is not just a goal—it's an ongoing journey … we provide an experience where clinical innovation is perfectly balanced with unbridled attention to care through our bespoke treatment plans. Our state of the art facilities, comprehensive laboratory assessments and transformational treatments reflect our dedication to unparalleled client experiences."
4. **Consultations page:** "Through one-to-one assessments, advanced health testing, and diagnostic analysis, our team builds a clear picture of your current health and skin profile. From there, we create a personalised plan across our Clinical, Nutrition, and Holistic Health teams – delivered with care, professionalism, and discretion."
5. **Fatigue & burnout page:** "we take a holistic and integrative approach … focusing on uncovering the underlying causes. Our treatments blend cutting-edge therapies, functional medicine, and lifestyle support to restore energy levels…"
6. **IV infusions page:** "our IV Infusions deliver a blend of essential nutrients, vitamins, and minerals directly into your bloodstream for maximum absorption and effectiveness. Whether you're seeking to boost your energy, improve recovery, or enhance overall wellbeing…" It also says: "Your appointment includes a brief 10 minute consultation with one of our doctors to ensure your selected IV infusion is appropriate."
7. **Immunity IV page:** "A blend of vitamin C and zinc enhances your body's ability to fight off infections and inflammation."
8. **NAD IV page:** NAD plays "a central role in energy production, cellular repair, cognitive function, and healthy ageing … widely used to promote energy, mental clarity, recovery, and overall wellbeing."
9. **Page titles shown in search results:** "Wellness & Longevity Clinic London | Acupuncture | The HVN" (home); "Contact The HVN | Expert Skin & Hair Clinic Support"; "Book Your Skin & Hair Treatment Appointment | The HVN".

Regulatory context [Reported]: the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) has ruled that IV nutritional therapy providers must not make medicinal claims, and that immune-boosting claims for IV drips were a problem in past cases. [Hypothesis] Some wording above may need review by a qualified adviser. This briefing makes no legal finding.

## Part D: Discussion prompt
> "Here's how The HVN describes itself (quotes above), plus three alternative taglines someone suggested. Which wording makes you trust them more, which makes you roll your eyes, and which would actually get you to book?"
