# Research Kit 03 · Consent, Privacy and Participant Welfare

**Owner:** data protection officer (DPO). **Must be approved before any recruitment** (AP10, AP11). Wording below is a draft for DPO and legal review, not legal advice.

## 1. Data-protection requirements

| Topic | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Controller | The HVN (confirm the legal entity name: the privacy policy says "The HVN Wellness Limited", the site says "The HVN Wellbeing Ltd"). The panel provider is a separate controller or processor for non-clients |
| Lawful basis | DPO to decide: likely legitimate interests (research to improve services) with an opt-in invitation. Record a legitimate-interests assessment if used |
| Special-category data | **Designed out.** Guides don't ask about diagnoses, medication or treatment outcomes. If a participant volunteers health details, don't probe; omit them from notes (write "[health detail omitted]") |
| DPIA | Run the DPIA **screening** below. If any answer is "yes", do a full DPIA before starting |
| Minimisation | Researchers receive first name (or alias) and session time only. No client records, Pabau data or contact lists |
| Recording | Audio or video only with separate opt-in. A notes-only option is always available |
| Storage | The HVN's own secure drive (access-controlled). No personal data in this Git repository |
| Pseudonyms | P-U01…P-U06 (usability), P-I01…P-I12 (interviews). The key linking names to IDs is held by The HVN only |
| Retention | Recordings deleted 3 months after the read-out; notes kept pseudonymised for 12 months; consent forms per The HVN's records policy |
| Rights | Withdrawal until the read-out is written (date stated). Access and erasure requests go to the DPO |
| Processors | Video tool, transcription service and panel: DPO checks UK GDPR terms. Prefer no automatic transcription, or a UK/EU-hosted tool |
| Quotes | Used only with consent, anonymised, with no detail that could identify a client |

### DPIA screening (yes/no)
1. Will we process health data on purpose? (Design target: **no**.)
2. Will we use recordings of faces or voices?
3. Will a new tool or processor be used?
4. Could any participant be vulnerable, e.g. in distress about their health?
5. Will data leave the UK/EEA?
6. Will client data be matched to research notes?

## 2. Participant information sheet (draft)

> **Help improve how The HVN explains its services**
>
> **What is this?** The HVN, a clinic in Knightsbridge, wants to understand how people find information and book appointments, so it can make its website and booking clearer.
>
> **What will I do?**
> - *Interview:* a 30-minute conversation about how you looked for and chose care.
> - *Website session:* a 45-minute session using the website on your phone while thinking aloud.
>
> We won't ask about your medical history or treatment. You won't be asked to book, pay or enter personal information on the website.
>
> **Do I have to?** No. You can stop at any time, skip any question, and withdraw your data until [date] without giving a reason. Taking part, or not, has no effect on any care you receive.
>
> **Recording:** only if you agree. You can choose notes only.
>
> **Your data:** stored securely by The HVN, identified by a code instead of your name, recordings deleted after 3 months. Quotes are used only without your name, and only if you agree. See [privacy policy link]. Questions: [DPO email].
>
> **Thank you:** £50 gift card or a charity donation (interviews), or the panel rate (website sessions).

## 4. Welfare and distress protocol
- **Before:** remind participants they can skip questions or stop.
- **If a participant becomes distressed:**
  1. Pause and offer to stop.
  2. Don't counsel or give clinical advice.
  3. Offer signposting: their GP, NHS 111, Samaritans 116 123.
  4. If there is immediate risk, follow The HVN's safeguarding procedure and call 999 if needed.
  5. Record only "session ended early: welfare" in the notes.
- **Clinical questions from participants:** "I'm not able to advise on that. The clinic team or your GP can help." Pass no details on without permission.
- **Complaints raised in an interview:** with the participant's permission, pass to The HVN's complaints process; keep them out of research notes beyond "complaint referred".
- **Moderators:** trained in this protocol. No clinicians as moderators.
