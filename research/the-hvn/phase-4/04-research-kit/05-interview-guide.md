# Research Kit 05 · Interview Guide: Clients and Non-Bookers (8–12)

**Needs approval:** AP11 (gate G3), DPO sign-off (Kit 03). **Not started.** Length: 30 minutes. One interviewer plus a note-taker, or recorded with consent.

**Structure:**
1. Open questions (no mention of price, CQC, membership, practitioners or setting).
2. Probes, only on themes not yet raised.
3. Stimulus last.

Code every note OBS / REP / OPN / HYP and mark it unprompted or prompted (Kit 01 §2).

## 0. Opening (2 min)
> Thank you for your time. This is about how people find and choose care, not about your health. I won't ask about medical details. If something comes up you'd rather not discuss, just say so. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing you say will affect your care. Is it OK to [record / take notes]?

## 1. Core questions: ask all, in this order (18 min)

| ID | Question | Follow-ups (neutral) | Aim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | "Think back to when you first started looking for [the kind of help you came to The HVN for / were enquiring about]. What was going on that made you start looking?" | "What did you do first?" | Trigger (REP) |
| **Q2** | "How did you go about choosing where to go? Walk me through what you looked at." | "What else?" · "Where did you look?" · "What made one option stand out?" | Decision criteria. Note **unprompted** mentions of price, practitioner, regulation, reviews, setting, membership |
| **Q3** | "Was there anything that nearly stopped you booking, or (for non-bookers) that stopped you?" | "Tell me more about that." · "What happened next?" | Barriers (REP) |
| **Q4** | "What did you expect your first appointment to give you?" | Clients: "How did that compare with what actually happened?" | Expectations vs experience |
| **Q5** | Clients: "After that first appointment, how did you decide what to do next?" · Non-bookers: "What did you end up doing instead?" | "What information did you have at that point?" | Plan decision (REP) |
| **Q6** | "If a friend asked you about The HVN, what would you tell them?" | "Anything you'd warn them about?" | Positioning in their words (OPN) |

## 2. Probes: only for themes **not** raised unprompted (6 min)

Ask each as an open question; **don't** suggest an answer.

| ID | Theme | Probe wording | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Cost | "How did you think about the overall cost when you were deciding?" → if relevant: "What would have helped you understand it?" | H01, H02 |
| P2 | Consultation fee | "How did you feel about paying for the first consultation?" | H03 |
| P3 | Practitioner | "How important was it to know who would be treating you? How did you find out?" | H04 |
| P4 | Regulation | "Did you look into whether the clinic was regulated, or check anything like that? What did you find?" | H05, H12 |
| P5 | Paying per visit | "Some places in London sell memberships; The HVN charges per visit. Does that make any difference to you?" (Ask only after Q2 and Q6) | H06 |
| P6 | Setting | "How much did the place itself (the setting, the atmosphere) matter compared with other things?" | H13 |
| P7 | Booking practicalities | "How did you actually make the booking or contact? How did that go?" | H09, H10 |

## 3. Segment modules (3 min: use the one that applies)

| Segment | Extra question |
|---|---|
| Women's health / fertility | "Were you also seeing a GP or specialist? How did you think about combining that with The HVN?" (No clinical detail.) |
| Aesthetics | "How do you usually decide who to trust with aesthetic treatments?" |
| IV / longevity / energy | "What would have to be true for you to commit to a longer programme?" |
| Gatekeeper (PA / family office) | "What do you need to check before booking for someone else?" |
| Non-booker | "Is there anything The HVN could have done differently that would have changed your decision?" |

## 4. Stimulus (2 min, last)
Show two short statements on screen (approved by compliance first):
- **A (current):** "London's leading Wellness & Longevity Clinic."
- **B (concrete):** "Doctor-led care in Knightsbridge. You'll know who treats you, what it costs, and there's no membership."

> "Which feels more believable to you, and why? Is anything missing?"

Code as OPN, prompted.

## 5. Close (1 min)
> "Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?"

Thank them, confirm the incentive, remind them of the withdrawal date.

## 6. Note-taker rules
- Write **verbatim** where possible. Mark paraphrases with ≈.
- Omit health details: write "[health detail omitted]".
- One row per statement in `templates/interview-notes.csv`, with question ID, evidence code, prompted/unprompted, theme and hypothesis ID.
- Within 24 hours: list the 3 strongest REP or OBS statements per interview.
