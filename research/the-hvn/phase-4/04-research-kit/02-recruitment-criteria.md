# Research Kit 02 · Recruitment Criteria and Screener

**Not started.** Recruitment of non-clients needs AP10 (gate G2). Any contact with clients or enquirers needs AP11 (gate G3). **Only The HVN contacts its own clients**: researchers never receive client lists.

## 1. Usability tests: 5–6 non-clients (via a UK research panel)

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 30–60 |
| Location | Lives or works in Greater London, or visits central London at least monthly |
| Recent behaviour | Has **paid privately** in the last 24 months for at least one of: aesthetic treatment, IV/wellness drip, private blood test or health check, acupuncture, menopause or women's-health consultation |
| Device | Will take part on their **own smartphone** (at least 4 of 6) or laptop |
| Spend comfort | Would consider spending £150+ on a first private appointment (self-reported) |
| Mix (quota) | 2 × women's health / perimenopause interest · 2 × aesthetics · 2 × energy/longevity/executive; at least 2 men across the sample |

**Exclusions:**
- Under 18.
- Works for a clinic, spa, health-marketing agency or competitor.
- Has been a client of The HVN.
- Took part in health-clinic research in the last 6 months.
- Currently receiving treatment for a serious mental-health crisis (see the distress protocol in Kit 03).

## 2. Interviews: 8–12 clients and non-bookers (The HVN recruits)

| Group | Number | Source (inside The HVN only) | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recent new clients | 4–6 | Pabau: first appointment in the last 6 months | Mix of entry points: aesthetic consultation, Discovery or nutrition, IV, acupuncture, blood test |
| Non-bookers (enquired, didn't book) | 3–4 | Enquiry log, contact forms or phone notes from the last 3 months | Only those whose enquiry consent covers follow-up contact; **data protection officer to confirm** |
| Gatekeepers (PA, family office) | 0–2 | Known bookers on behalf of others | Only if they exist |

**Quotas:** at least 2 women's-health or fertility, 2 aesthetics, 2 IV/longevity/energy. At least 1 international or visiting client if available.

**Exclusions:**
- Under 18.
- Current complaint or dispute with The HVN.
- Staff, relatives of staff, or partners.
- Clients whose care currently involves acute distress (clinician judgement).
- Anyone who asks not to be contacted for research.

## 3. Screener (panel, 2 minutes)

| # | Question | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | What is your age? | 30–60 |
| S2 | Where do you live or work? | Greater London / monthly visits |
| S3 | In the last 2 years, which of these have you paid for privately? (multi-select list) | At least 1 |
| S4 | Do you, or does anyone in your household, work for a clinic, spa, beauty or health business, or a marketing agency? | No |
| S5 | Have you ever been a client of The HVN in Knightsbridge? | No |
| S6 | Can you join a 45-minute video call using your own smartphone, and share your screen? | Yes |
| S7 | Which topic is most relevant to you right now? (perimenopause/menopause · skin/aesthetics · energy/longevity · none) | Assign quota cell |

S7 is about interest only. **Do not ask about diagnoses or medical history.**

## 4. Invitation templates (for The HVN to send after approval: **not sent**)

**Client / non-booker invitation (email from The HVN):**
> Subject: Help us improve how The HVN explains its services
>
> We're asking a small number of people to share their experience of finding and booking care with us. It's a 30-minute video or phone conversation with an independent researcher. There are no right or wrong answers, we won't ask about your medical details, and taking part (or not) won't affect your care in any way. As a thank-you we offer a £50 gift card or a donation to a charity of your choice. If you're interested, reply to this email and we'll send an information sheet. — The HVN Concierge Team

**Panel brief:** "45-minute remote session looking at a London clinic's website on your phone. We won't ask about your health. Incentive per panel rate."

## 5. Logistics
- Track candidates in `templates/screener-tracker.csv` using **pseudonymous references only**. Names and contact details stay in The HVN's or the panel's system.
- Over-recruit by 1 for usability and 2 for interviews (no-shows).
- **Incentives are neutral:** a gift card or charity donation, **never** a treatment credit or discount.
