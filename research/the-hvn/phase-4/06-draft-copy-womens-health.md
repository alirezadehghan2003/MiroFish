## Drafting principles
1. **List only services verified on the live site** (15 Oct 2026), with the prices and durations shown on their own pages.
2. **No efficacy claims.** Use "support", "may", "some people", never "treats", "cures" or "effective for".
3. **No prescription-only medicine names** (the current PCOS and PMDD pages name metformin, SSRIs and contraceptive pills). If medical treatment is offered, describe it generically ("medical options where clinically appropriate") **only if the clinic actually prescribes**, and only as cleared by compliance.
4. **Signpost to the client's GP or specialist**, and to urgent help where symptoms may be serious (PMDD).
5. **Plain English; British spelling** ("oestrogen").
6. **No acne, rosacea or scarring treatments** on these pages.
