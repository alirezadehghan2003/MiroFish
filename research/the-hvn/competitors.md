## 1. How to read this file

### 1.1 Evidence and confidence

| Label | Meaning | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **[LIVE-VERIFIED 2026-10-15]** | Loaded directly from the source on that date | High |
| **[OFFICIAL-PR]** | The company's own press release, as found by search | Medium–High (accurate at publication; may have changed) |
| **[TRADE/PRESS]** | Independent trade or lifestyle publication | Medium (dated; may be promotional) |
| **[DIRECTORY]** | Aggregator or directory listing | Low (often unsourced or stale) |
| **[V1-INDEXED]** | Competitor's own page as summarised by a search engine in the v1 research (Oct 2026); **not re-verified** | Low–Medium |
| **[HYPOTHESIS]** | Analyst inference | Not evidence |

### 1.2 Access limitation (read first)

On 15 October 2026 every competitor website, the CQC and ASA registers, and review platforms were **blocked by this environment's network policy**. Only the-hvn.com could be loaded. Every competitor fact below therefore comes from search results, press releases or directories, and **no competitor price is [LIVE-VERIFIED]**. Before any figure in this file is used externally, it must be checked on the competitor's own site. The domains to allow for that are listed in §7.

### 1.3 Price rules
- Prices are "as published" on the date shown. They are not quotes.
- Where sources conflict, all values are shown.
- No price is estimated or inferred.

## 2. Relevance tiers

Not every longevity clinic is a direct competitor. Each provider is tiered by how much of The HVN's **proposition** it overlaps: an integrated Knightsbridge spa-clinic hybrid combining medical aesthetics, IV and wellness therapies, TCM and acupuncture, functional and longevity testing, and luxury rituals.

| Tier | Definition | Who it matters for |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Direct** | Same micro-location (Knightsbridge/Belgravia) **and** a hybrid spa + medical/longevity proposition at a luxury price | All segments |
| **2. Longevity substitutes** | Medical- or diagnostics-led longevity offers elsewhere in central London, often membership-based | Longevity Optimiser, Burnt-Out Executive |
| **3. Service-line specialists** | Compete for one service line (aesthetics, IV/HBOT, diagnostics, women's health, acupuncture), not the whole proposition | Aesthetics-First, Women's Health, single-treatment buyers |
| **4. Substitutes** | Hotel spas and clubs competing for the same "wellbeing spend" occasion | Holistic Seeker, International Visitor |
| **Partner** | Levitas, which delivers the 8R programme at The HVN | Channel relationship, not a competitor |

## 7. Comparative findings

| # | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Membership is the dominant model among Tier 1–2 rivals** (Surrenne, Lanesborough, Lanserhof, Pillar, Hooke; HUM2N monthly). The HVN publishes **per-visit and per-test prices** and has no live membership or programme price except 8R "from £3,000". | Matrix above + HVN live site |
| C2 | **The HVN's published single-service prices sit within the London range** where comparable data exist: NAD £350 vs £295–£450 (HUM2N) and from £350 (IV Drip Clinic). The HVN is not a price outlier on IV. | V1-INDEXED competitor data; HVN live |

The HVN retired, or at least unpublished, its £14,500 / 16-week 360° programme. **[HYPOTHESIS]** Whether that was a pricing, demand or operational decision is unknown; ask management. | Pillar press release; HVN 404 |
