## P6 · Margaret Hughes · likes 10

A short due-diligence reminder for anyone assessing private health and wellness services. Before relying on a provider, verify: CQC registration and whether an inspection has taken place; GMC registration for each named clinician; written terms of service; price lists with a publication date; indemnity insurance; data protection arrangements; and the complaints procedure. Where a service is unrated or has not been inspected, we treat that as an open question rather than a neutral fact. Subject to verification in every case.

I know too little about Surrenne and HUM2N to compare them fairly, and anything I've read about prices or status may be out of date, so I'd check the current CQC record and ask for a written quote before booking anywhere.
