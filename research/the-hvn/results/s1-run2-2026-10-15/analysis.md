## 3. Implications [HYPOTHESIS], ranked by evidence strength
1. **"Who will treat me" on every service page.** Replace the site-wide team carousel with treatment-specific practitioners and registration numbers (insight 1; verified gap).
2. **State the fee-credit rules for every consultation in one place** (£175, £350, £400) (insight 4; verified gap).
3. **Turn the CQC badge into a verification link**: registered status, inspection pending, regulated activities (insight 2; audit W7).
4. **Publish indicative total-pathway costs**, or a priced first-step bundle such as the hypothetical S2b (insight 5; audit W3/W8).
5. **Evidence notes and tier differences on IV/NAD pages**, after regulatory review of claims (insight 3; audit W4).
6. **Position "no membership" as a feature** with a simple break-even explainer (insight 7; test in S4).

## 5. Data-quality caveats (read before using any insight)

| Issue | Example | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Invented experience about a real competitor** | Sofia (C9): says Harrods charged her again for a consultation without crediting it | **Excluded.** A fabricated anecdote, not evidence about Harrods |
| Factual slips | Khalid and Claire: CQC registration means "registered and inspected" (The HVN is **not yet inspected**); Helena and Isla (interviews) attribute a "membership" or "fixed fee" to The HVN (it has none); Tom refers to the Medical Director as "he" (the seed gives no gender) | Corrected in this analysis; next seeds should state "no membership" and "not yet inspected" more prominently |
| **Memory truncation** | camel agents cap context at 8,192 tokens; 4 of 12 interviewees (Sofia, Claire, Daniel, partly Helena) said they "don't know" The HVN | Interview answers are weighted below thread content |
| Homogeneous, cautious voice | Most agents write as diligent analysts ("I have not been…", catchphrases such as "Paperwork before the promise", "Show me the panel") | Insights reflect what evidence-minded buyers would ask, not emotional or impulse buyers |
| Seeded framing | Opening posts (generated from the brief) set the four topics | Insights 1–5 partly follow the seeded topics; 6–10 emerged in replies |
| Small sample | 12 agents, one thread set, one model | Hypotheses only; validate with 8–12 real client interviews |

## 6. Why the MiroFish auto-report is not used
The report agent retrieves facts from the **Zep knowledge graph**. With graph memory updates disabled (required for this run), **simulated posts and comments never enter the graph**, so the auto-report states that it found "no recorded participant thread" and that its own interview tool "failed". It reasons only from the seed and repeats seed-level errors (e.g. it links the two phone numbers to the 2025 blog post). It cost $0.048. **Recommendation:** for S2–S4, skip the MiroFish report (saving about 25% of the cost) and use this evidence-checked analysis from the raw database and interviews. Alternatively, enable graph memory updates if you want the auto-report to see the discussion; that needs a separate decision because it sends simulated content to Zep.

## 7. Method notes
- **09:00 start:** OASIS has no start-hour option. All hour lists in `simulation_config.json` (agents' active hours, peak and off-peak hours) were shifted by −9, so round 1 behaves as 09:00 and round 24 as 08:00. OASIS's own log still prints the unshifted index ("Day 1, 00:00"). Before and after files are in `raw/`.
- **English:** `Accept-Language: en` on all MiroFish calls. 11 profile `country` values ("英国", "阿联酋") and one persona typo were corrected to English before start (`raw/data_fixes_log.txt`; originals in `raw/*.as-generated.json`).
- **No real people as agents:** three nodes (a real person's role, a generic "London GP" duplicate and an empty "WellnessJournalist" placeholder) were deleted from **this run's** graph before profile generation. Pilot 1's graph and results are unchanged.
- **Safety:** all 126 LLM requests went through the budget proxy, which holds the only copy of the key. The backend held a placeholder (direct calls 401). A Sonnet request was refused at preflight.
