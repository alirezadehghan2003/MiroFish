## P8 · Daniel Okafor · likes 4

A short checklist I am using before paying for any health programme or membership, in case it helps others comparing options: (1) the total cost in writing, with the price of each stage; (2) what is included and what is excluded; (3) whether a first consultation fee is credited against later spending; (4) renewal terms and the cancellation notice period for each item; (5) the name and professional registration of the clinician who carries out each step.
